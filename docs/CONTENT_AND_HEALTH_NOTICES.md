# Content, Age Rating, and Health Notices

## Editorial intent
Herbal Line is an educationally framed management game about the work and responsibility involved in a regulated medical-cannabis supply chain. Cannabis is explicitly named in the setting. The game does not promote intoxication, recreational consumption, or home production.

## Age rating
The team may target an age rating such as 16+, but must not claim that rating is guaranteed by adding a warning label. The final rating depends on the content and the applicable rating authority/platform. Review the finished game against the target storefront's current requirements before release.

## Suggested opening notice
> Herbal Line is a fictional management simulation set in a regulated medical-cannabis supply chain. It explores staffing, documentation, quality assurance, traceability, compliance, and logistics. Production values and workflows are abstracted for gameplay and are not instructions for real-world cultivation or processing. Medical information is educational only and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified health professional.

## Contextual notice principles
- Use notices at relevant decision points, not as repetitive interruptions.
- Distinguish fictional simulation outcomes from real-world facts.
- Explain that medical-cannabis authorization, product status, and rules differ by jurisdiction.
- Avoid implying that cannabis is harmless or that a particular product is suitable for everyone.
- Do not provide diagnosis, dosing, treatment selection, or instructions for changing a prescribed treatment.
- If real medical or research claims are included, cite a current, reputable source in the game's reference panel and include a review date.
- Clearly separate general research summaries from established clinical conclusions; mention uncertainty where evidence is limited.
- Do not imply that gameplay success predicts real clinical outcomes.

## Example contextual notices
**At campaign start:** “This simulation represents a fictional regulated operation. All production variables are normalized game values.”

**At a quality decision:** “Batch status reflects fictional in-game quality records. Real products are subject to applicable testing and regulatory requirements.”

**At a research unlock:** “Research findings vary by condition, product, and study quality. This entry is educational and does not provide individual medical advice.”

**At pharmacy dispatch:** “This game's operational scope ends at documented delivery to a pharmacy. It does not simulate dispensing or patient treatment.”

## Research content workflow
1. Write a narrow factual claim.
2. Find an appropriate primary or official medical/regulatory source.
3. Record the source title, URL, publication/update date, and review date.
4. Have the wording reviewed for accuracy and non-promotional framing.
5. Avoid overgeneralizing from one study or presenting preliminary findings as established treatment guidance.
6. Re-check time-sensitive legal and medical content before release.

## Visual and narrative guardrails
- Focus on professional work, stewardship, quality, safety, and accountability.
- Do not reward consumption or intoxication.
- Avoid patient testimonials or dramatic medical outcomes unless carefully sourced, consented, and reviewed.
- Avoid using disclaimers as a substitute for responsible content design.
